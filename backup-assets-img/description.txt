# Dusk Coffee — Product Photo Ideas

## 1. Arabica Coffee

**Single Origin Reserve**
A clear kraft pouch of whole coffee beans standing upright, beans spilling slightly onto the surface in front of it, a few loose beans and a small cluster of red coffee cherries beside the bag to hint at natural fruity notes, warm terracotta surface —

**Light & Bright Roast**
A light tan paper bag of pale, light-roast coffee beans standing upright with the top folded down, a glass pour-over dripper and a citrus wedge placed beside it to suggest bright acidity, beans scattered lightly on a pale wood surface —

**Night Owls Decaf**
A matte dark-navy coffee bag standing upright, a crescent moon motif subtly embossed on the bag (no text), whole dark roasted beans spilling from the top, a small votive candle glowing softly beside it, deep near-black surface —

---

## 2. Robusta Coffee

**High-Octane Espresso**
A deep-black coffee bag standing upright, glossy dark roasted beans spilling out, a small espresso cup with thick golden-brown crema placed beside the bag, a few cocoa nibs scattered near the cup, dark slate surface —

**Morning Hustle Pack**
A stack of 3-4 individually wrapped drip-coffee sachets fanned out on a desk-like surface, one sachet torn open with ground coffee visible, a plain white ceramic mug positioned just behind them, warm morning-toned lighting, minimal office props (a pen, a folded notebook corner) —

**Traditional Kopi Tubruk**
A small kraft paper pouch of very finely ground dark coffee powder, folded open at the top to reveal the fine grounds, placed beside a traditional small glass tumbler with a spoon of condensed milk swirling in, a few sugar cubes nearby, rustic wooden surface —

---

## 3. Liberica Coffee

**The Rare Jackfruit**
A cream-colored coffee bag standing upright with whole large asymmetrical Liberica beans spilling out, a few slices of fresh ripe jackfruit arranged beside the bag for aroma context, warm golden surface with soft tropical light —

**Sweet Aftertaste Blend**
A tan coffee bag standing upright, a visible mix of two bean shapes (rounder Arabica and larger irregular Liberica) spilling from the top, a small dish of honey or light caramel drizzle placed beside it, soft warm-toned surface —

**Liberica Cold Brew**
A tall glass bottle of dark cold brew concentrate with visible condensation, a few ice cubes scattered beside it, a single glass filled halfway with cold brew over ice in the background slightly out of focus, cool blue-toned side lighting against the dark background —

---

## 4. Excelsa Coffee

**The Wild Tart Berry**
A deep maroon coffee bag standing upright with whole beans spilling out, a small handful of wild berries (blackberries/blueberries) scattered beside the bag to hint at the tart flavor profile, moody dark surface with a single warm highlight —

**Mystic Blend**
A dark violet-black coffee bag standing upright, beans spilling from the top, an espresso shot glass with dark crema placed beside it, a few dried berries and a single cocoa pod fragment arranged nearby to suggest the bitter-to-tart-sweet profile, dramatic low side lighting —

**Excelsa Coffee Pods**
A small open box of coffee capsules with 2-3 pods placed neatly beside the box, one pod standing upright in front, a compact home espresso machine partially visible and softly blurred in the background, clean modern dark surface —
